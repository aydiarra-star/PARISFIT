PARISFIT is a physical-activity support application project with a3D coach. We are preparing an independent review of a dossier concerning AB01 / Squat / repetition1 and its Static/1 reference, from the public dataset by Alizadeh Noghani, Green and Bolívar-Nieto (2025), DOI10.7274/28689221.v1.
